Perovskite solar cell autoencoder: a 20 ms J–V simulator

A PyTorch re-implementation of the conditional autoencoder in Zbinden, Comi, Knapp & Tress, Autoencoder for parameter estimation and current-voltage curve simulation of perovskite solar cells, npj Comput. Mater. 12, 7 (2026), trained on the authors' public data. Everything runs in your browser.

Move a slider: the decoder redraws the transient J–V curves at six scan speeds.

Pick a simulated device from the paper's test set (never used in training). The encoder estimates its six parameters; the decoder then rebuilds its curves from the estimate.

What this is. The paper's network (two convolutional and three dense layers per half, six latent parameters), retrained in PyTorch on the authors' simulated Solaronix devices: FTO / TiO₂ / m-TiO₂-MAPI / m-ZrO₂-MAPI / carbon, 1 sun, scan speeds 100 to 0.005 V/s. One training run (fold 3, early-stopping patience 200).
Limits. The decoder is only valid inside the parameter ranges of the paper's Table 3 and for this device architecture. The estimates are point values without uncertainty, and different parameter sets can give very similar curves (as the paper notes). Weights are compressed for the web: decoder int8, encoder float16.
