How to read this.
  • One curve cannot decide. Several parameter sets fit it about equally well. Two solver fits that put the recombination at different interfaces differ by only 0.03 mA/cm² RMS within one assumed model. Measurements at several light intensities would separate them.
  • The network has no HTL-side traps. It was trained before we found that HTL-side traps fit better, so its sliders cannot reach the HTL solver fit. A retrained model is in progress.
  • The decoder has its own error ( mA/cm² on average on unseen simulated devices). So a decoder curve that looks close is not proof: the decoder fit's parameters give a larger RMS when re-simulated in the solver (dashed line).
  • The encoder leaves the training range on the measured curve (shunt resistance far above the range). We read that as a sign that the model is missing something, not as a measurement.
  • Calibration: a light-loss factor of 0.845 matches Jsc for the missing glass and generic optical data, so Jsc is not evidence, and optical errors can pass into τ and μ.
